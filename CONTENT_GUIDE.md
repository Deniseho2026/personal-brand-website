# Denise Ho Website — Content Editing Guide

The website has two complementary content layers. The **Content Studio** is the everyday place to add new work, upload images, publish articles, and read contact enquiries. The public pages are bilingual, so each item is stored in English and Hong Kong Traditional Chinese together. The established design direction is documented in `ideas.md` for future visual changes.

## Opening the Content Studio

Go to **`/studio`** from the website’s address, then sign in with the account that owns this website. The Content Studio is restricted to the site owner. It has a central editor for new work and a right-hand column for core page copy, saved content, and recent enquiries.

| What you want to change | Where to do it | What happens after saving |
|---|---|---|
| Add an article, painting, project, or collection | **Add new item** | The item remains private as a draft or appears publicly when published. |
| Change an existing article, artwork, project, or collection | **Content library** | Selecting an item loads it back into the editor. Save again to update it. |
| Change biography paragraphs or service descriptions | **Core page copy** | The revised English and Chinese copy appears on the public About or Services page. |
| View contact-form messages | **Recent enquiries** | New messages are shown with name, enquiry type, and email address. |

## Publishing a New Article

Choose **Article** under *Type*, then write a clean URL slug such as `midlife-and-meaning`. The slug becomes the end of the article address: `/writing/midlife-and-meaning`. Add a title, category, introduction, and full article in **both English and Traditional Chinese**. You may add comma-separated tags and an image. Choose **Draft** while you are working, then change the publishing status to **Published** and save when the article is ready.

Published articles automatically appear on the Writing page. Each article opens in its own calm, long-form reading page. If the English or Chinese full-content field is empty, the short introduction will still be available as a minimal public fallback, but both versions should normally be completed before publishing.

## Adding Artwork, Projects, and Collections

For a painting, choose **Artwork**, enter both titles, and add the year and medium in each language. The visible Artwork gallery reads the image, title, year, medium, and short introduction from the saved item.

Use **Project** for a specific completed undertaking, such as an exhibition, workshop, university session, or community art project. Use **Collection** for a thematic group of artworks or writings, such as *Inner Landscapes* or *Psychology Notes*. Both appear in the Projects & Collections section of the Art page after they are published.

> **Practical distinction:** Projects describe *what you have done*. Collections describe *how you organise work around a theme*.

## Replacing or Adding Images

Use the **Upload image** control in the Content Studio. It accepts JPG, PNG, or WebP files up to 5 MB. When the upload completes, a preview appears in the editor. Save the content item afterward to associate the image with that article, artwork, project, or collection. To replace an image, upload a new file while editing the existing item; to remove the current image, use **Remove image** and save.

The initial site includes custom studio and watercolour placeholders. Replace the hero portrait and other initial placeholders with Denise’s own artwork and photography before public launch. The current visual system is intentionally quiet so that personal imagery remains the main source of colour.

## Editing Biography and Service Text

On the right side of the Content Studio, open **Core page copy**. Select the section you want, write the English and Traditional Chinese versions, then save. Available fields include the About-page opening paragraph, four main biography sections, and the five service descriptions. This keeps core writing editable without disturbing the visual layout.

Services for **individual counselling** and **expressive arts groups** retain their Hong Kong-only labels in the page structure. The professional registration disclaimer is likewise kept as an intentional, fixed legal-safety element. Review any material legal or professional-positioning changes with an appropriate professional adviser before altering related public wording.

## Bilingual Content Management

The language switch toggles the complete site between English and Hong Kong Traditional Chinese. Every field that is editable in the Content Studio appears in a paired English/Traditional Chinese form. This avoids mechanical translation and allows each language to read naturally. Before publishing, verify that neither language field is blank and that the titles, categories, media labels, and descriptions correspond to one another.

## Contact Enquiries

The contact form asks for name, email, optional phone number, enquiry type, message, and consent. It stores the message securely in the website database and makes it visible in the owner’s **Recent enquiries** panel. No email service is needed for the form to function; however, it does **not** currently send automatic email alerts. Check the Content Studio regularly, or arrange an email-notification integration before launch if immediate email alerts are required.

The form includes a privacy-friendly notice. It should not be presented as an emergency channel, and visitors are asked not to submit urgent, medical, or highly sensitive information.

## Pre-Publication Review

Before publishing, replace every placeholder that needs a personal image, proofread English and Traditional Chinese content side by side, test the language switch, and check all service descriptions and Hong Kong-only labels. Submit a test contact enquiry and confirm that it appears in the Content Studio. Finally, review the Facebook and Instagram links, confirm the phone number and email address, and test the website on a phone and a desktop browser.

When the content is ready, create a website checkpoint and then use the **Publish** control in the management interface. A custom domain can be connected later from the domain settings.

## Homepage Journey and Ways of Working

The Content Studio now includes two additional content types for the homepage. Choose **Journey story** for the personal thread running through Denise’s work: writing, counselling, expressive arts practice, and painting. Published Journey stories appear in **Home → A connected journey**. Use the English and Traditional Chinese title fields for the card heading, the short-introduction fields for the visible card text, and the optional image upload for a small supporting image.

Choose **Work record** for records of how Denise works with people, groups, organisations, universities, communities, or other partners. Published Work records appear in **Home → Ways of working**. Their short introductions become the card descriptions and their optional images appear above the card text.

Use **Display order** to control the sequence in each homepage section. Lower numbers appear first. A draft is stored in Content Studio but stays off the public homepage. Change **Publishing status** to **Published** and save again when the item is ready. If no published Journey story or Work record exists, the original starter cards remain visible as a fallback.

| Content Studio type | Public destination | Recommended content |
|---|---|---|
| Journey story | Home → A connected journey | A personal story about writing, counselling, expressive arts, or painting. |
| Work record | Home → Ways of working | A record of work with people, groups, organisations, or communities. |

The existing **Core page copy** panel remains for the About page biography and Services descriptions. It does not edit the homepage journey cards or ways-of-working cards.
